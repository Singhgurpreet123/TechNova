Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries regarding our tech services, pricing, and project workflows.

TechNova Solutions specializes in end-to-end IT services, including custom Web Application Development, Native/Cross-Platform Mobile Apps, UI/UX Design, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning integration, Cloud Infrastructure DevOps (AWS/GCP), and Data-Driven Digital Marketing.

We offer transparent pricing options tailored to your needs. For continuous support and cloud hosting, we offer monthly subscription tiers ($19/mo, $49/mo, $99/mo). For custom software projects, we provide clear fixed-scope milestone estimates following an initial discovery consultation.

Delivery timelines depend on scope. Standard web projects and turnkey product configurations take approximately 2–4 weeks. Complex enterprise software systems with custom AI algorithms typically take 6–12 weeks, with functional prototypes delivered every 2 weeks via Agile sprints.

All TechNova clients receive 24/7 technical monitoring and helpdesk access. Depending on your service tier, we offer guaranteed SLAs ranging from 24 hours down to 1 hour for urgent production issues, along with regular software security updates and database backups.

We maintain a 14-day full satisfaction money-back guarantee for all monthly subscription tiers. For custom software development projects, cancellation policies are governed by milestone sign-offs outlined clearly in your master service agreement.
